Rooted on the North Shore

Port Jefferson is home.

The harbor has been doing business since the shipyards of the 1800s. So have our neighbors.

Port Jefferson grew up as a working harbor. The village once called Drowned Meadow took its current name in 1836, built ships for the Atlantic trade, and has sent a ferry across the Sound to Bridgeport since 1883. It is still a town of owner-run shops, restaurants, trades and professional practices, and those are the businesses we know best.

We serve clients throughout Suffolk and Nassau counties, including Port Jefferson, Port Jefferson Station, Belle Terre, Mount Sinai, Miller Place, Setauket, Stony Brook, Rocky Point, Smithtown and Huntington, and we work with clients across New York and beyond through secure online file sharing.

Long Island has its own tax terrain: New York State and New York City returns for commuters, the MCTMT for self-employed Suffolk and Nassau residents, school and property tax questions, and multi-state filings for anyone splitting the year with Connecticut or Florida. We handle all of it every season.
