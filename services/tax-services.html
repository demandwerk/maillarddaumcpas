Tax Services

Filing a return records what already happened. Planning shapes what happens next. We prepare accurate, fully supported returns, and we meet with clients during the year to find the decisions that still change the result.

Long Island clients face their own tax terrain: New York State and City returns for commuters, the MCTMT for the self-employed, residency questions for people splitting time with Florida or Connecticut. We work through these every season.

What we handle

  • Individual returnsFederal 1040 with New York and other state returns
  • Business returnsC corporations, S corporations, partnerships and LLCs
  • Fiduciary, estate and gift returnsPrepared with our trusts and estates practice
  • Tax planning and projectionsYear-end moves, estimated payments, retirement contributions
  • Multi-state and residencyAllocations, part-year returns, domicile changes
  • IRS and NYS noticesResponses, audits, payment plans and penalty abatement
  • Amended and prior-year returnsCatch-up filing for missed years
  • Nonprofit returnsForm 990 series and New York CHAR500

How an engagement works

  1. Gather

    We send a personalized organizer and secure upload link with exactly what we need.

  2. Prepare

    Your return is prepared, then reviewed by a partner line by line.

  3. Review together

    We walk you through the result and anything that changed from last year.

  4. Plan ahead

    We set estimates and a mid-year check-in so next year has no surprises.

Who we serve

Individuals and families, business owners, commuters, retirees, landlords, and anyone who has received a letter from the IRS or the NYS Department of Taxation.
