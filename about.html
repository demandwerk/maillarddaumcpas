About Us

Two partners, one harbor-village practice.

Maillard CPAs is becoming Maillard & Daum. The name is new. The care, the continuity and the phone number clients rely on are not.

Our story

Built one client relationship at a time.

[Partner first name] Maillard founded the practice in [year] with a simple idea: people deserve an accountant who knows them, picks up the phone, and explains things clearly. That idea grew into a firm serving individuals, closely held businesses, securities professionals, and the trustees and executors who look after family assets across Long Island.

As the practice grew, so did the need for a second partner who shared those values. [Partner first name] Daum joins as partner, and the firm becomes Maillard & Daum, CPAs. Clients keep the same office, the same team and the same attentive service, with more depth behind every engagement.

We are deliberately a small firm. That lets us keep a partner involved on every file, meet clients in person when it matters, and stay close enough to the work to catch what larger firms miss.
